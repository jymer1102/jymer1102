Hi, I'm jymer1102

I'm a creator, designer, gamer, streamer, pilot, businessman, triathlete, and Christian. I love to use my skills to help and entertain others.

The One And Only

Jackson Weimer, otherwise known as jymer1102, or just "J", was born and raised in Springfield, Missouri. He is the first one in his family to do lots of the things he does to this day. Such as: triathlon, aviation, development, and coffee.

Academics

J grew up going to New Covenant Academy and the Phelps Gifted Education program in elementary. His sophomore year, he transfered to Logan-Rogersville High School. J's goal is to be accepted into the United States Air Force Academy to pursue his dream of becoming a fighter pilot.

Athletics

Like many kids, J grew up doing sports. As he got older, he tried out triathlons. He fell in love. He has competed in them ever since and competed in USA Triathlon Nationals when he was 15. His goal is to make it to the Iron Man World Championships in Kona, Hawaii.

Development

J grew up adjusting and exploring new technology and software. He came to love the idea of learning how things work the way they do, and making them do that. He developed multiple apps. The reason? Because he wanted to learn, and he wanted to make something personal.

Flight

J grew up loving planes. When he turned 14, he went on his first discovery flight, and he fell in love with aviation. He has been flying ever since. His goal is to become a fighter pilot in the United States Air Force.

Religion

Growing up, J was surrounded by Christian parents, friends, and mentors. Eventually, in the summer of 2023, he chose to give his life to Christ. J tries to incorporate his love and passion for Christ into his work and everything he does the best he can.
