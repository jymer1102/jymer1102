This Privacy Policy explains what information jymer1102.com (the “Site”) collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. The Site is a personal website and portfolio run by Jackson Weimer, who also goes by jymer1102 (“I”, “me” or “my”). By using the Site you acknowledge this policy. If you do not agree with it, please do not use the Site.

1. The short version

  • The only personal information I receive is what you choose to send me through the contact form or by email.
  • The Site currently has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking cookies.
  • Your browser stores two small items for the Site: your light or dark theme, and your cookie choice.
  • I do not sell your personal information.

2. Information I collect

2.1 Information you give me

The contact form asks for your name, email address, a subject and a message, plus anything else you choose to write. If you email me directly, I receive your email address and whatever you send. Please do not include sensitive information (such as health, financial or government ID details) in a message.

2.2 Information collected automatically

When your browser loads the Site it sends standard technical information, such as your IP address, browser type and version, device and operating system, the page you asked for, the page you came from, and the date and time. This is received by my hosting provider (GitHub Pages) and by the third-party services listed in Section 5 that deliver fonts and code libraries to your browser. I do not run analytics on the Site or build visitor profiles from this information.

2.3 The code playground

Code you type into the Playground stays in your browser. It is not sent to me, not stored by me, and not saved after you leave, unless you choose to download it. The live preview runs in your browser, and if your own code asks for files from other websites, those requests go from your browser directly to those sites.

Your choices

  • You can accept or decline optional cookies in the banner, and change your answer at any time with .
  • If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, the Site treats it as a request to decline optional cookies.
  • You can also block or delete cookies and local storage in your browser settings. The Site will still work, but it will forget your theme.

4. How I use information, and why

  • To reply to you. I use what you send through the contact form or email to read and answer your message. (Legal basis under GDPR: steps you request, and my legitimate interest in responding.)
  • To run and protect the Site. Technical data is processed by my hosting and delivery providers so the Site loads and stays secure. (Legitimate interests.)
  • To remember your choices. The two items in Section 3. (Legitimate interests, as they are strictly necessary.)
  • To follow the law. Where a legal obligation requires it.

I do not use your information for automated decision-making or profiling.

5. Services that help run the Site

These providers process data on my behalf or deliver content to your browser. Each has its own privacy practices:

  • GitHub Pages hosts the Site. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Formspree receives and processes contact form submissions and forwards them to me by email. See the Formspree Privacy Policy.
  • Google Fonts serves the Site’s web fonts. Google receives your IP address and browser details when the fonts load. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • jsDelivr delivers the icon libraries used on the Site. See the jsDelivr Privacy Policy.
  • unpkg delivers the scroll-animation library, and cdnjs (Cloudflare) delivers the file-zipping library used by the Playground’s download button. See the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

The Site also links to other places, such as Linktree, Gmail, and projects hosted on GitHub Pages, Render and Base44. Once you follow a link, that site’s own privacy practices apply, and I am not responsible for them.

6. Sharing and selling

I share personal information only with the providers in Section 5 as needed to run the Site and the contact form, when the law requires it, or to protect the rights, safety or property of me or others. I do not sell your personal information, and I do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising (as those terms are defined under California law). In the past 12 months I have not sold or shared personal information.

7. How long I keep information

I keep contact messages for as long as I need to respond and for reasonable record-keeping, then delete them. Formspree keeps submissions under its own retention schedule. The theme and consent items stay on your device until they expire or you clear them.

9. International transfers

I am based in the United States, and the providers in Section 5 process data in the United States and other countries. If you visit from outside the United States, your information may be transferred to and processed in countries whose data protection laws differ from yours. Where required, providers rely on safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

10. Children

The Site is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets a higher age), and I do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has sent me personal information, email me and I will delete it.

11. Security

I take reasonable steps to protect the information I receive, and the Site is served over HTTPS. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, so I cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. Changes to this policy

I may update this policy from time to time. When I do, I will change the effective date at the top of this page. If a change is significant, I will also ask for your cookie choice again.
