Hi, I'm jymer1102

I'm a creator, designer, pilot, triathlete, and Christian. I love to use my skills to help and entertain others.

The One And Only

Born and raised in Springfield, Missouri, Jackson "J" Weimer (jymer1102) has always charted his own course. From aviation and software development to triathlons and an unapologetic obsession with coffee, he's blazing a trail entirely his own.

Academics

A product of New Covenant Academy and currently a student at Logan-Rogersville High School, J’s academic sights are set sky-high. His ultimate mission? Earning a spot at the United States Air Force Academy to realize his dream of becoming a fighter pilot.

Athletics

What started as standard childhood sports quickly evolved into a relentless drive for endurance. Since competing in the USA Triathlon Nationals at just 15, J has locked his sights on the ultimate test of human limits: the Ironman World Championship in Kona, Hawaii.

Development

Driven by a burning curiosity to understand how things work, J transforms ideas into digital reality. He doesn't just use software—he builds it. Whether developing custom apps or exploring new frameworks, he codes to learn, create, and push the boundaries of tech.

Flight

A childhood fascination with airplanes became a lifelong calling at age 14 after a single discovery flight. Since taking the controls, J hasn't looked back. Today, every hour in the cockpit is a deliberate step toward his ultimate goal of flying fighters for the U.S. Air Force.

Religion

Rooted in a strong community of family and mentors, J fully dedicated his life to Christ in the summer of 2023. This faith isn't just a background belief—it's the driving force and foundation that guides his work, his passions, and his daily life.
